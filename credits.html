Art & Audio

The app icon, splash screen, player archetype sprites, and stadium/field backdrops were created with the help of AI image-generation tools. Some sound effects and ambient audio were created with the help of AI audio-generation tools. Both were then selected, edited, and integrated by hand to fit the game's look and feel.
