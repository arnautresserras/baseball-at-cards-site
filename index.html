Baseball, dealt one card at a time.

A roguelike run: chart your route, draft your lineup, beat every boss.

Download on theApp Store

  1. 1 Chart your season

    Each run lays out a branching map. Play it safe for Peanuts, or take the boss gauntlet for stronger recruits. The route is yours.

    Game
    Beat a regular pitcher for Peanuts.
    Boss
    An ace who bends the rules. Win Peanuts and a new recruit.
    Shop
    Spend Peanuts to sign Pros and All-Stars, the only place to fix a shaky roster before the finale.
    Event
    A choice with a catch: Peanuts, a gamble, a new player or a reworked card.
    Train
    A drill that lasts the whole run: your Swing, Ball or Strike cards get a chance to upgrade.

  2. 2 Draft All-Stars

    Recruits come in packs of Pros and All-Stars, and the pitch before the deal tells you what’s inside. A ball that clears the wall means an All-Star.

    • Sluggers
    • Small-ball surgeons
    • Patient walkers
    • Count-controllers

    Over a dozen archetypes, and every card in your deck comes from the nine batters you pick.

  3. 3 Every one cheats

    Every ace bends the rules. One hides his stamina, one heals on strikeouts, one deals your cards face-down. Read the matchup and draft your counter.

Daily Run

A new run every day: the same map, opening pack and Run Rule for everyone. One attempt. Free players get one every 7 days; the one-time purchase opens it daily.

Run Rules

Each Daily Run bends the game with one of 13 hostile rules, from Deep Fences to Pitcher’s Ump, on top of every opponent’s own.

Game Center

Required for the Daily Run. A daily leaderboard, an all-time Best Score board for every run, friend challenges, and all 21 achievements synced (2 new).

Walk it off

Free to start: jump into a free Sample Game anytime, then get unlimited Survival Runs with a single one-time purchase. No subscriptions.
