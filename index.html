◆ PLAY BALL ◆

How it plays

Every pitch is a card. Every run is a new map. Route through the regular season, load up for the playoffs, and see how far your lineup can go.

1
🃏

Tap or drag to Play

Pitches, swings, steals, and plays are cards in your hand. Read the count, pick your card, and make it happen — no clutter, just baseball.

2
🗺️

Choose Your Path

Each run lays out a branching map of games, bosses, shops, and events. Play it safe for cash, or take the boss gauntlet for stronger recruits — the route is yours.

3
🧢

Draft Your Lineup

Recruit from over a dozen player archetypes — sluggers, small-ball surgeons, patient walkers, count-controllers. Every card in your deck comes from the nine batters you pick.

4
⚾

Bosses With a Trick

Every ace bends the rules — one hides his stamina, one heals on strikeouts, one deals your cards face-down. Read the matchup and draft your counter.

5
🥜

Earn & Spend

Win fights to stack up Peanuts, then spend them at the shop to sign the players you actually need — the only place to fix a shaky roster before the finale.

6
🔥

Free to Start

Jump into a free Sample Game anytime. Unlock unlimited Survival Runs with a single one-time purchase — no subscriptions.

◆ NEW IN 1.1 ◆

Every run keeps score

Version 1.1 turns a finished run into a record: a full scorecard, a Run Score, an all-time board, and something to show for it.

📋

The Scorecard

Win or lose, every run ends in a full card: your slash line, runs, walks, outs spent, the fights you took down, and the ace who finally ended you.

🏆

Run Score & All-Time Board

Runs are scored on the fights you win, the variety of ways you drive runs in, and how few outs it cost you — scaled by your on-base percentage. Your best 25 runs are kept, ranked.

📤

Share Your Card

Turn any scorecard into an image and send it anywhere — straight from the run-end screen or from the board.

🏅

19 Achievements

From Beat the Game to Murderers’ Row, Surgical, and Hall of Fame — tracked with progress bars, most tied to one archetype’s way of playing.
